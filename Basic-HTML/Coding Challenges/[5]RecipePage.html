Chopsuey

savory vegetable and meat stir-fry

Ingredients

  • 150g chicken breast (sliced),
  • 150g pork belly (sliced)
  • peeled boiled quail eggs
  • broccoli (cut into florets)

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a pan or wok. Sear the chicken and pork until browned and cooked through. Remove and set aside.
  2. In the same pan, sauté the garlic and onion until fragrant and soft.
  3. Add the firmest vegetables first (carrots, cauliflower, broccoli) and stir-fry for 2 minutes. Add the quicker-cooking vegetables (cabbage, snap peas, young corn).
  4. Return the meat to the pan. Pour in the water or chicken broth, fish sauce, and oyster sauce. Bring to a boil.
